# CMS Energy

> CMS Energy is a Michigan energy holding company whose principal subsidiary, Consumers Energy,
> is a regulated electric and natural-gas utility. Two public API surfaces exist. One is
> first-party and anonymous: an Esri ArcGIS Server publishing Consumers Energy outage and
> service-territory geodata over REST and SOAP at www.consumersenergy.com/arcgispublic. The
> other is the Green Button Connect My Data (GBCMD) customer-data program, which Consumers
> Energy operates on the UtilityAPI EE/DER Engagement Platform and which requires third-party
> registration plus a customer's OAuth authorization.

## Anonymous first-party API — outage and service territory

No key, no registration. ArcGIS Server 11.5.

- Service catalog: https://www.consumersenergy.com/arcgispublic/rest/services?f=json
- Server info: https://www.consumersenergy.com/arcgispublic/rest/info?f=json
- CEOutageMap (MapServer, 7 layers): outage jobs with JOB_ID, CUSTOMER_COUNT, CAUSE_CD,
  CAUSE_DESC, OUTAGE_TIME, EST_TIME_RESTORATION, EST_TIME_ETR, CREW_ASSIGNED; layer 0 is the
  electric service territory polygon.
- ServiceDashboard (MapServer + FeatureServer, 5 layers): CE_County_Outages (layer 3) and
  CE_City_Outages (layer 4) rollups, plus CustomersInterruptedPercent, NumberOfInterruptions
  and NumberOfWireDowns.
- Query pattern: `/services/<service>/<Server>/<layerId>/query?where=1=1&outFields=*&f=json`.
  maxRecordCount is 1000 per response; page with resultRecordCount / resultOffset.
- SOAP: every map service also publishes a WSDL 1.1 contract with 55 operations at
  `/arcgispublic/services/<service>/MapServer?wsdl`, and the catalog publishes 7 more.
- No OGC extension. supportedExtensions is empty on CEOutageMap and "FeatureServer" on
  ServiceDashboard, so there is no WMS/WFS GetCapabilities.
- Errors come back as `{"error":{"code","message","details"}}`, sometimes under HTTP 200 — parse
  the body, not the status.

## Gated first-party program — Green Button Connect My Data

- Base: https://utilityapi.com/api/v2 (JSON) and
  https://utilityapi.com/DataCustodian/espi/1_1/resource (Green Button REST / OpenESPI, Atom XML).
- Register with Consumers Energy as a third party to be assigned a client_id. Registration
  begins in sandbox mode; Consumers Energy reviews it and emails you when you may go live.
- Customer authorization is Green Button OAuth 2.0 authorization code. Default customer
  authentication is Consumers Energy single sign-on (scope value `auth-sso`); the test scenarios
  `test_residential`, `test_commercial` and `test_empty` are selectable with
  `AdditionalScope=auth-test-<scenario>`.
- Three ESPI token classes: registration_access_token (registration endpoints),
  client_access_token (authorization and bulk endpoints), access_token (resource and batch
  endpoints). All are sent as `Authorization: Bearer`.
- Resources: authorizations, meters, bills, intervals, files, events, accounting
  (billing-accounts, billing-summaries), templates, forms.
- Rate guidance: at most 1 bill or interval download per minute; at most 1 of everything else
  per second. Exhaustion returns 429 with Retry-After. A 202 with Retry-After means the payload
  is being built asynchronously.
- Error envelope: `{"error", "error_description", "url"}`. Not RFC 9457.
- Timestamps are ISO 8601. UTC by default, but values parsed off bills and intervals carry the
  utility's LOCAL timezone.
- Webhooks are opt-in, HMAC-SHA256 signed, retried hourly for 72 hours, and NOT ordered. 23
  documented event types.
- Revoking an authorization deletes collected data and access credentials. It cannot be undone
  from the third-party side; the customer must authorize again.

## Standards

- Green Button Connect My Data — Consumers Energy states it is certified by the Green Button
  Alliance as compliant.
- NAESB ESPI 1.1 (OpenESPI) — the resource tree, object model and Atom XML are ESPI.
- U.S. Department of Energy DataGuard Energy Data Privacy Program — stated compliance.
- OAuth 2.0 authorization code and client credentials.
- RFC 9116 security.txt at https://www.consumersenergy.com/.well-known/security.txt
